Compact status or category label for table cells, list rows and headers.

```jsx
<Badge color="success" dot>Active</Badge>
<Badge color="warning">Due in 1 week</Badge>
<Badge color="info" appearance="solid">New</Badge>
<Badge color="neutral" appearance="outline">Draft</Badge>
```

- `color`: `neutral` (default) · `info` · `success` · `warning` · `danger`
- `appearance`: `soft` (default tint) · `solid` · `outline`
- `dot` adds a status dot; `square` swaps the pill for a small radius.
